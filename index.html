Welcome to Paradigm Realm

A playable early alpha focused on building your civilisation on Earth.

  1. Your selected Settler can found a city. Press B or use its action button.
  2. Click a unit to select it; click an adjacent square to move. Arrow keys also move. Forests and hills cost 3 movement, open ground 2, connected roads 1.
  3. Select a city to choose production. Food grows its population; production builds units and improvements automatically.
  4. Workers build roads. Research Agriculture to build farms on grass or desert.
  5. Choose research and end turns. Rivals explore, settle and produce armies. Declare war before attacking them.
  6. Advance through Electricity, expand your settlements and develop your empire. Eliminate rival cities and units to win by conquest, or keep building peacefully.

Scroll the map, use the overview, or change zoom. Tab selects the next unit. Enter ends the turn. Save stores in this browser; export a save to keep a portable copy.

Alpha scope: generated Earth-like geography, two rivals, six technologies, simple diplomacy and AI. No naval travel, multiplayer, religion or exact Earth geography yet.
