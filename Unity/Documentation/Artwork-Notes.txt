The existing concept sheets are retained as scaled gallery pages. The 21 terrain
assets use the corresponding existing terrain illustrations, resized with nearest
neighbour sampling for game use.

New atlas generation used the built-in ImageGen tool, not the API/CLI fallback.
The generated transparent atlases were sliced into individual 128x128 textures.
Manual crop boundaries were used where the generated sheet had uneven gutters.
No image-generation service or subscription is needed to run the game.

PROMPT SET
1. Production transparent 4x4 atlas matching the prior 16-bit unit/city references:
   settler, worker, stone warrior, scout; archer, mounted knight, rifleman, spearman;
   horseman, catapult, medic, trade wagon; wheat, iron, gold, spices. Shaded pixel
   art, three-quarter view, upper-left lighting, no text, isolated silhouettes.
2. Production transparent 5x4 city atlas based on the prior Stone Age sheet:
   five distinct architectural families in Stone, Bronze, Dark and Medieval ages.
   Hide tents, roundhouses, adobe, stilt huts and turf huts advance to palisades,
   classical settlements, desert citadels, Asian castles and European fortresses.
   No labels or borders; miniature shaded pixel art with transparent surroundings.
3. Production transparent 3x3 scenery atlas: deciduous trees, pines, snowy mountain;
   wheat farm, mine entrance, stone fort; tent village, brown horse, jungle palms.
   Consistent isometric-looking pixel art, isolated silhouettes, no labels.

IN-GAME MAPPING
Unit indices match World.UnitNames exactly (0 through 11).
Resource indices match World.ResourceNames (1 through 5).
City rows 0..3 are selected by Bronze Working, Iron Working and Feudalism.
City columns 0..4 vary deterministically by owner and city ID.
The original graphics-only release retained gameplay rules. The current update
keeps its square grid and artwork; see Design/IMPLEMENTATION-STATUS.md for the
new terrain-rendering and city/AI changes.
Future technology concepts and realm scenes are gallery references only.
