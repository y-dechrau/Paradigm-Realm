PARADIGM REALM — SETTLEMENT STRATEGY, STAGE 2
For Unity 6000.3.25f1. Includes the graphics overhaul and Stage 1 terrain/city improvements.

INSTALL
1. Close Unity. Back up your current project and saved games.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy the WHOLE Assets folder into your existing project, merge and replace:
   C:\Users\Matth\Downloads\Paradigm-Realm-Unity\Paradigm-Realm-Unity
4. Copy Design and Documentation too if you want the notes beside your project.
5. Reopen Unity. Run Paradigm Realm > Run Engine Checks and Run Graphics Checks.
6. Open Paradigm Realm > Terrain Transition Review. Click Render diagnostic map.
7. Press Play. Load a saved game or start a game. Open a city and try its focuses.

Do not copy just RealmGame.cs: this update includes several new C# files.
Packages and ProjectSettings are included for opening a separate project copy.
You do not need to replace these folders in your existing project.

WHAT TO TRY
- Select a Settler and choose Compare settlement sites.
- Compare food, production, fresh water, resources, upkeep and unknown surroundings.
- Click Travel to... to issue an ordinary movement order. Use B to found manually.
- Hover explored tiles with a Settler selected for benefits and founding blockers.
- River centres provide 3 food / 2 production; hill centres 2 food / 3 production.
- Fresh water allows population 8 before an Aqueduct; other sites allow 6.
- Aqueducts allow 12 and sewers allow 20, with no extra stacking water bonus.
- Watch rivals seek good colony sites and delay expansion when money is low.

CHANGES TO EXISTING SAVES
Version 6 saves continue to load; this stage adds no serialized fields; no world regeneration or asset GUID changes.
Existing terrain, cities, technology and orders are retained on load.
Existing cities immediately receive their river/hill/fresh-water site benefits.
Worker allocation follows the improved food-aware rules immediately.
City food storage is capped at the next population threshold when a turn runs.
A granary preserves half that threshold after growth. Existing large cities remain.
The city focus integer now also supports Commerce (4). Keep a backup when trying
new versions; reverse compatibility with older game builds is not guaranteed.
