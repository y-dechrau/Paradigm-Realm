PARADIGM REALM — ALPHA 0.7

1. Stop Play mode. Back up your Unity project.
2. Extract the ZIP and open Paradigm-Realm-Unity, then Assets.
3. Copy Scripts, Editor and Resources (including all .meta files).
4. Paste them into your EXISTING project Assets folder.
5. Merge folders and replace matching files. Do not paste inside Scripts.
6. Return to Unity and wait for import/compilation.
7. Select Paradigm Realm > Run Engine Checks.
8. Press Play. Load an old save or start a new game.

New games include exploration sites. Older saves retain their existing map.
Economy: governments, luxury spending, trade income.
City screen > Citizens / specialists: assign specialists.
City production panels: Rush buttons and new buildings/wonders.
Cities: atlas, military adviser, wonder list.
Caravan unit panel: trade with a nearby city or supply a wonder.
Diplomacy: gifts, technology purchases and peace offers.

Actual Unity playtesting is still needed. See ALPHA-0.7-README.md.
