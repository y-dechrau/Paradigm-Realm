## (b) Ranked improvements

Ranking weighs impact on mid-game engagement against effort. Rules use integer values to match the game's style; all are starting numbers to tune.

1. **Event cards with 2 choices (Old World, Stellaris anomalies).** Medium effort, highest payoff. Each turn, per civ, roll a 1-in-12 chance (once per 12 turns on average) for an event drawn from a list of about 40 conditioned on game state (drought on desert tile city, plague at size 8+, a wandering scholar, a border dispute with a neighbour). Each has two choices with costs and gains in gold, food, shields, content citizens (e.g. "Pay 40 gold: +2 content in that city for 10 turns" or "Refuse: -1 content for 5 turns"). Add a memory flag per city so later events can refer back (Old World's memory idea). Store as a fixed-size ring of the last 8 events and write them to a "Chronicle" panel, which also gives DF-style history for free (item 9).
2. **Era score and Golden / Dark ages (Civ VI).** Medium. Award points for achievements: found a city +2, discover a keystone tech +3, complete a building tier +1, win a battle against a stronger unit +2, meet a civ +1, convert a city +2, finish a wonder +4. At each era completion compare the total with thresholds 30, 60, 90 times era number (tune): below low gives a Dark Age for the next era (-1 content per city, but pick a "recovery" bonus), above high gives a Golden Age (+1 Trade per worked tile with Trade, +25% shields for 10 turns), a Dark then Golden swing gives Heroic (both effects, plus a free edict). The game already has an era-completion edict hook (`Edicts.cs`), so this fits there.
3. **Barbarian / independent peoples camps (Civ VI, Humankind, Civ VII).** Medium. Place camps on unclaimed land with a spawn cooldown of 15 turns. A camp that sees a unit nearby spawns a scout; when the scout returns, raiders (2 to 4 units of the player's era minus one) spawn at 1 in 3 per turn. Destroying a camp gives 25 gold times era. Tribal villages already exist: add a rule that a village not joined within 40 turns becomes a camp, and a camp cleared by a civ can be turned into a village that joins that civ (Humankind's absorb option). Also gives auto-explore and combat units something to do early. Do not let camps spawn inside borders or within 4 tiles of a capital.
4. **Unique unit and building per civilisation (Civ V/VI, AoE).** Small-to-medium. Each of the 12 premade civs gets one unique unit replacing a base unit (+1 Attack or +1 Defence, or +1 move, same cost) and one unique building replacing a base building (+25% of its output, or a minor extra). Content only, no new systems. Pair with the existing two traits so each civ has a recognisable playstyle. Keep custom civs able to pick one from a short list.
5. **AI agendas (Civ VI).** Small. Give each AI leader one like and one dislike from 8 (large navy, many cities, wonders, religion spread, high military, peace, trade, science). Each turn a visible fact matching the agenda changes that civ's attitude toward you by +1 or -1 (clamped -20 to 20). Show the reason in the diplomacy screen ("dislikes your large army"). This makes the new diplomacy screen readable, and explains war declarations.
6. **Great People via points (Civ V/VI).** Medium. Specialists, libraries, temples and wonders add Great Person points (1 per specialist per turn). Threshold: 100 for the first, +60 each after, per type (Scientist, Engineer, Merchant, Prophet, General). Each can be spent (free tech step, instant building, 150 gold, found/boost a religion, a general giving +2 attack to a stack) or placed to give a permanent tile improvement. Cap by civ at one active at a time. Makes specialists attractive and gives names for a chronicle.
7. **Religious unit roster (see section c).** Medium. Inquisitor, apostle-style upgrade for the missionary, and a monk or priest that can heal and calm a city.
8. **Fame / score race across eras (Humankind) as the Transcendence alternative.** Small. Show a visible score that sums era-score points, cities, techs and wonders; at turn 300 (or Transcendence) the highest wins a "Score victory". Provides a clear goal when conquest and Concord stall. Fame is Humankind's sole victory metric (confirmed in the Wikipedia article).
9. **Dwarf Fortress-style generated history log.** Small once item 1 exists. Append a single line per notable event (city founded, city lost, war, faith founded, wonder built, golden age) with year and names. At game end show "The History of the Realm" and export it as text. Name the ages after the leading civ, as DF does (confirmed in the search result).
10. **Dark age loyalty-lite: unrest from conquered cities (Civ VI loyalty, Humankind stability).** Small-to-medium. A conquered city starts with 6 turns of resistance (no production), -2 content if a former owner's religion or culture dominates. Reduces snowballing by conquest that Stage 6 victory rules otherwise make quick. Marked lowest because the existing disorder rules already do part of this.

## (c) Priest / religious unit designs

- **Civ V:** Missionary (spreads religion, limited charges, loses strength in foreign-religion territory), Inquisitor (removes other religions from a city, defends against missionary action), Great Prophet (founds or enhances religion, spreads strongly). Pressure and units are separate.
- **Civ VI:** Missionary (3 spreads), Apostle (4 spreads, promotions chosen from a list like Debater, Proselytizer, Translator, Martyr (unsure exact list)), Inquisitor, Guru (gives faith bonuses, unsure) and theological combat between religious units. Religious units can be purchased with faith. Good idea to copy: charges and combat between rival faiths.
- **Civ IV:** Missionary built in a city with the religion's monastery; no pressure.
- **AoE II:** Monk converts enemy units and heals friendlies, carries relics to a monastery for gold, is fragile and easily killed by cavalry. AoE IV: monks and holy site relics give gold. Good idea to copy: relics as an objective on the map (fits ancient ruins).
- **Suggested for Paradigm Realm:** (1) Missionary: 3 charges, +10 pressure to the target city per charge, cost 40 shields. (2) Inquisitor: unlocks with the faith's first temple, removes 20 pressure of every other faith in a city, and halves incoming missionary effect for 10 turns; may be a defensive stance. (3) Priest (unit or specialist): in a city, +1 content per temple, and moved to a battlefield heals adjacent units by 2 per turn (AoE monk). (4) Relics: ruins sometimes yield a relic; carrying it to a temple gives +2 faith per turn (AoE). (5) Apostle: Missionary upgrade after 2 successful spreads, 5 charges, can start a new pantheon variant. Mark as design inspiration, numbers are my proposals.
