# National forces (first release, 20 Nov 2026)

Cities build buildings. The civilisation raises, refits, reinforces and keeps its soldiers and warships on **Empire > Forces**.
One existing map unit is one *formation*; movement, stacking and combat are unchanged.

## Which units

- **National formations:** Warrior, Spearman, Archer, Horseman, Knight, Rifleman, Catapult and War Galley (`ForcesRules.Service` > 0).
- **City-trained, as before:** Settler, Worker, **Scout** (kept in cities so early exploration needs no programme), Medic, Caravan, Boat, Galley and Priest.
- Starting units, villages and ruins are unchanged.

## Capabilities and where the numbers live

All values are in `ForcesRules` (Forces.cs).

| Capability | Rule | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Service capacity | `BaseService` + citizens x policy share | 2 + 50% peacetime, 100% mobilised |
| Service per formation | `Service[]` | Warrior/Spearman/Archer 1; Horseman/Knight/Rifleman/Catapult 2; War Galley 3 |
| Arms output | `BaseArms` + `CityArms` per city + `WorkshopArms`/`FactoryArms`/`BarracksArms` + the Empire military priority (trade to arms, only while the programme has work), then +`IronPercent` with connected Iron | 3, 1, 3/5/1, 25% |
| Horse support | `HorsesPerSource` per connected Horses pasture (or a treaty import); Horseman and Knight use 1 | 2 |
| Mobilisation | `MobiliseShieldPercent` of every city's shields; policy stands `PolicyMinTurns` | 25%, 10 turns |
| Over capacity | `ShortfallGold` per point a turn; orders adding personnel blocked | 2 |
| Programme | `MaxOrders` | 8 |

- **Arms costs:** `CostFor` of the unit, which applies speed, civilisation and difficulty, exactly as cities paid in shields.
- **Refit cost:** the larger of a quarter of the new price and the difference in price.
- **Replacing losses:** half the new price for each lost health point.

## Orders (`ForceOrder`, saved in `Nation.forceOrders`)

| Kind | What it does |
|---|---|
| Raise (0) | Reserves service and horse support at approval. Spends arms each turn, top order first, with any surplus flowing down. Deploys at its city: land formations on the city tile, warships at the port launch tile. If the city is lost or full, the formation is **held** with its commitments; the player chooses another city ("City ▸"), and rivals redeploy at once. Upkeep starts when it stands on the map (the existing `BudgetDetail`). |
| Refit (1) | Upgrade paths as before: Warrior → Spearman → Rifleman, Horseman → Knight. The formation must stand in your city and is locked (`Unit.refitting`: no moves or attacks) until done. It keeps its id, name, victories, rank and health. A mounted formation keeps its own horse slot, so it refits even when every slot is committed. |
| Replace losses (2) | A damaged formation in your city is restored to full health when the arms arrive. Ordinary healing continues meanwhile and costs nothing; there is one order per formation, so nothing is paid twice. |

- **Cancel:** releases the reservations, which are read from the list so they are released exactly once, and unlocks the formation. Arms already spent are lost; the confirmation says so.
- **Disband:** removes the formation. Its service and horse support are free at once; its equipment and experience are lost; nothing is refunded. A confirmation shows all of this.

## Mobilisation

- **Mobilising:** capacity becomes all citizens; every city loses a quarter of its shields, visible in the city yields and in the preview (capacity and total shields before and after).
- **Timing:** each change must stand 10 turns, so toggling never gives free capacity.
- **Demobilising over capacity:** no formation is removed. The shortfall shows on the Forces screen, costs 2 gold a point each turn, and blocks orders that add personnel until you are back within capacity.

## Changing circumstances

| Event | What happens |
|---|---|
| Lost horses | Mounted formations stay; no new mounted orders; infantry unaffected. |
| Fall in population | Shortfall as above. |
| Lost deployment city | The order is held, with no duplicate. |
| Formation killed while refitting | Its order ends and releases its reservations. |

A soldier put on a city's unit line by any old path (auto-manage, the imperial priority, an older plan) is removed each turn (`ForcesTurn`).

## Rivals (`ForcesAI.cs`)

Once a turn a rival:
- mobilises at war when short of personnel, and stands down at peace after the minimum;
- keeps at most three orders, each one `RaiseReason` allows;
- raises towards its old soldier limit (`SoldierLimit`) with the strongest formation it can field (every fourth one ranged, mounted only with free support), at the city nearest an enemy, else its capital;
- otherwise refits one formation.

The old per-unit gold upgrades (`AIUpgrade`) are off under national forces.

## Saves

- **Save version 44.** An older save is migrated (`MigrateToForces`): a soldier in training on a city line becomes a Raise order keeping its shields as arms; soldiers further down the queue are dropped (nothing had been paid for them).
- **New games** from the menu turn national forces on. `World.New` alone (as used by the checks) leaves them off, so older checks keep city training.

## Not built (room left for it)

- Allied contingents from treaties or gates. A support category beside horses would follow `HorseSupport`/`HorsesCommitted`.
- Naval support as a specialist category; warships currently need only a port city.
- Early levies and professional armies.
- Buying arms with gold.
